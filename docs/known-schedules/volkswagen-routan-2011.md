# Volkswagen Routan, 2011

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-routan-2011.json` was made. The rules it follows are in
[known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Routan card read, and what isn't covered, is on the
[2013–2014 page](volkswagen-routan-2013-2014.md).

| Model year | What was read | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 2011 | "2011 USA Volkswagen Maintenance Schedule by Miles - Routan Only" (revised November 2013), in full, as page images; the list was written from it. `MC-10242442` and `MC-10212707` are the same card | `2013/MC-10156036-9999` |

The card is an older grid than 2013's: 15 columns, 8,000 to 120,000, and no oil change indicator block.

- Oil and filter, and tire rotation, in every column: reminders every 8,000. **The card prints no oil change
  indicator wording and no months**, so the grid's 8,000 is the card's own interval and the reminders are
  shipped. The 2013–2014 card puts both under the indicator instead, and Volkswagen's bulletin `MC-10124405`
  (2014) speaks of the Routan's "change oil" indicator for 2009–2014; whether that should take the 2011 oil
  reminder away is in the open question on #452. No months (#517: none recorded).
- Engine air filter at 32,000, 64,000 and 96,000: reminder every 32,000.
- "If using your vehicle for dusty or off-road conditions: inspect the engine air cleaner filter" at 16,000,
  48,000, 80,000 and 112,000: table-only, named with its condition (an inspection).
- "Replace the air conditioning filter (if applicable)" every 16,000 to 112,000: table-only with no `service`,
  as every "(if applicable)" row is (#33). The 2013–2014 card has the cabin filter with no such mark and makes
  it a reminder there.
- Brake linings (`brake-inspect`), and front suspension, tie rod ends and boot seals, every 16,000 to 112,000;
  CV joints every 24,000; the exhaust system at 16,000 (footnote 1: "first inspection at 16,000 miles or 12
  months"), 24,000, 48,000, 72,000, 96,000 and 120,000. All table-only.
- **Engine coolant at 104,000**, footnote 2: "at 104,000 miles or 60 months (5 years), whichever occurs first".
  Reminder every 104,000 miles or 60 months, named with the card's figures.
- Automatic transmission fluid and filter at 120,000: reminder every 120,000; under Severe every 64,000, from the
  "police, taxi, fleet or frequent trailer towing" row drawn at 64,000 (that row stays in the table with its
  condition and no `service`, as on the [2013–2014 list](volkswagen-routan-2013-2014.md)).
- Spark plugs at 96,000: reminder every 96,000. PCV valve inspected at 96,000. Accessory drive belts at
  120,000: reminder every 120,000.
- Left out: the "Carefree Maintenance Program Coverage" shading and the "**" / "***" notes on coverage and the
  emissions warranty.
- **It differs from the [2012 card](volkswagen-routan-2012.md) in one mark**: the 2011 card draws the exhaust
  system inspection at 24,000 as well; the 2012 card doesn't. Otherwise the two are row for row the same.
