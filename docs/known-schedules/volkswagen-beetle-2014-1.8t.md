# Volkswagen Beetle 1.8T, 2014 (A5)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-beetle-2014-1.8t.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Beetle card read, how the Beetle and Beetle Convertible
cards are put into one list, and what isn't covered are on the [2014 2.5L's page](volkswagen-beetle-2014-2.5.md).

| Model year | What was read | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 | The Beetle 1.8T card (posted February 2014), in full; the list was written from it | `2014/MC-10122260-9999` |
| 2014 | The Beetle Convertible 1.8T card (posted February 2014), in full: the same rows, marks and footnotes, with the two convertible roof rows | `2014/MC-10122259-9999` |
| 2014 | Volkswagen's 2014 reissue, for the oil change's time limit only (see the 2.5L's page) | reissue `USA5R505521` |

- **The card is the [2014 Beetle 2.5L](volkswagen-beetle-2014-2.5.md)'s grid with the spark plugs at 60,000 and
  120,000** (footnote, "every 6 years") in place of 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000. Every other row has the same
  marks in the same columns, the automatic gearbox's row at 50,000 and 100,000 included. It is also the
  [2015 Passat 1.8T](volkswagen-passat-2015-1.8t.md)'s grid, with the convertible's roof rows.
- The convertible roof rows are table-only, named for the Beetle Convertible (see the 2.5L's page).
- `"engine": { "label": "1.8T", "displacementL": 1.8 }`.
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; tire rotation 10,000; dust and pollen filter 20,000 / 24 mo;
  automatic gearbox fluid 50,000 (not for a manual); spark plugs and air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; brake fluid
  every 24 months by time alone (the card says at 3 years first, then every 2).
- **Oil 12 months**, from Volkswagen's 2014 reissue (the owner's ruling of 2026-10-28, #517). The table has none.
- Left out: the dealer's service sticker, the empty timing belt row, and the grey "Carefree Maintenance" shading.
- **Not covered:** the 2015 and 2016 1.8T, whose Beetle cards draw the sunroof drain check at 20,000 where the
  convertible's don't (see the 2.5L's page; asked on #448).
