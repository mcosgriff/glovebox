# Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, 2025

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2025.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Atlas Cross Sport card read, and what isn't covered, is on the
[2020–2021 page](volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2020-2021.md#every-atlas-cross-sport-card-read).

Source: **Volkswagen's reissued maintenance card** for 2025, `USA5R701521`, printed 12/03/2025, from its
owners-literature site (`…/v1/document/760f5f9a-0290-4dfe-adf0-30d03981a9ad`), read on 2026-10-29, as page images,
by eye. No card from the time was found (NHTSA's index has none), so the reissue is the only source (the owner's
ruling of 2026-10-23).

- **The list is the [2023–2026 Atlas](volkswagen-atlas-2023-2026.md)'s without the automatic's fluid**, with the
  model's name. The card's levels name no model; of its additional items, the Atlas Cross Sport is named on one:
  **spark plugs every 80,000 miles or 6 years**, "Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, GLI, GTI, Taos, Tiguan (RM1)"
  (80,000 / 72 months). No engine is named.
- **No gearbox reminder.** "Transmission, Automatic - Change Fluid & filter (if applicable)", every 80,000 miles,
  names "09H: Atlas, Tiguan (RM1)" and "0GC (DSG): GTI, Golf R, Jetta, Taos"; the 09U severe-use row names "Jetta,
  Taos, Tiguan (RM1)". None names the Atlas Cross Sport, so it is left out, as the
  [2023–2026 Jetta GLI](volkswagen-jetta-gli-2023-2026.md) leaves out a DSG row that doesn't name it. The 2026 card
  adds "Atlas Cross Sport" to the 09H line, so the [2026 list](volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2026.md) has it. Asked on
  #453.
- **"AWD Clutch - Change fluid"**, "All Applicable Vehicles": "Change AWD clutch fluid (AWD)", every 36 months by time
  alone, in `allWheelDriveOnly`. NHTSA's safety-rating list for 2025 (`api.nhtsa.gov/SafetyRatings`, read 2026-10-29)
  has "2025 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport SUV AWD" and "… FWD". The reading waits on
  [#437's question 5](https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox/issues/437#issuecomment-6491507764).
- Left out as on the Atlas's list: the front axle differential lock fluid, the belt starter-generator belt, the tire
  mobility kit bottle, the inspections (the sunroof drains row names "Atlas", not the Cross Sport) and the Canada-only
  tire rotation.
- vPIC's model list for Volkswagen, 2025 (read 2026-10-29), names it "Atlas Cross Sport".
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; AWD clutch fluid every 36 months by time alone;
  brake fluid every 24 months by time alone (USA: 3 years after registration first); dust and pollen filter
  40,000 / 24 mo; spark plugs 80,000 / 72 mo.
