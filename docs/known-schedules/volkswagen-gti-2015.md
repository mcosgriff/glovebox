# Volkswagen GTI, 2015 (Mk7)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-gti-2015.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md).

Source: Volkswagen's "Maintenance Schedule For USA" cards, which Volkswagen files with NHTSA as
manufacturer communications (`https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/<year>/MC-<id>-<suffix>.pdf`; the ids
are in NHTSA's index at `https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/ffdd/tsbs/`). Read on 2026-10-23.

| Model year | What was read | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 2015 | The GTI 2.0TFSI card (posted June 2014), in full; the list was written from it | `2014/MC-10120120-9999` |
| 2016 | The GTI 2.0TFSI card (posted August 2015), in full. It differs from 2015 (below), so it isn't in this list | `2015/MC-10123072-9999` |

- **A list of its own for the new generation.** The 2015 card has the same jobs in the same columns as
  the 2014 GTI card (`2013/MC-10155950-9999`) that the
  [2010–2014 list](volkswagen-gti-2010-2014.md) was checked against, so this list's table and reminders
  are the same as that list's. The 2015 car is the Mk7, so it isn't added to the Mk6's years.
- The card is a grid: 12 columns, 10,000 to 120,000 miles, with no months printed for a column. Jobs
  "regardless of mileage" (airbag check yearly, the dust and pollen filter every 2 years, brake fluid,
  the tire mobility kit, spark plugs and the air filter every 6 years) are drawn in the columns
  Volkswagen chose and named with their time here where the time is the only thing that says when.
- Reminders: engine oil and tire rotation 10,000; dust and pollen filter 20,000 / 24 mo; DSG fluid and
  filter 40,000 (not for a manual car); air filter and spark plugs 60,000 / 72 mo; brake fluid every
  24 months by time alone (the card says at 3 years first, then every 2).
- One engine: the card is for the 2.0TFSI, the GTI's only engine, so the list names none.
- No special operating conditions are given on the card.
- Left out: the dealer's service sticker.
- NHTSA's index also files `2014/MC-10240819-9999` under the GTI; it is the 2015 Golf 2.0L TDI's card and
  wasn't used.
- **Not covered:**
  - **2016.** Its card drops tire rotation, the yearly airbag check, the door check lubrication, the rear
    washer nozzle check and the service sticker, and gives the dust and pollen filter every 40,000 miles or
    2 years (at 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000), so it has a list of its own,
    [volkswagen-gti-2016](volkswagen-gti-2016.md), found by vPIC's "Golf GTI" too.
  - **2017 to 2019** have a list each: [volkswagen-gti-2017](volkswagen-gti-2017.md) (from Volkswagen's
    reissue), [volkswagen-gti-2018](volkswagen-gti-2018.md) and [volkswagen-gti-2019](volkswagen-gti-2019.md).
    2020–2021 have [volkswagen-gti-2020-2021](volkswagen-gti-2020-2021.md).
