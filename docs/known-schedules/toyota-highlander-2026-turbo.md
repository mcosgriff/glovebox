# Toyota Highlander 2.4L turbo, 2026

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/toyota-highlander-2026-turbo.json` was made. The rules it follows
are in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md).

Source: Toyota's 2026 Warranty and Maintenance Guide for the Highlander,
`https://assets.sipb.toyota.com/publications/en/omms-s/T-MMS-26Highlander/pdf/T-MMS-26Highlander.pdf`,
which Toyota's owners site lists for the 2026 Highlander. Downloaded on 2026-10-22 and read on 2026-10-23.

- **A chart**, laid out as the 2025 one (printed pages 38 to 43: columns every 5,000 miles from 5,000 to
  150,000, a standard and a "Special Operating Conditions" chart for each run of ten). It was compared cell
  by cell with the 2025 chart, which was read in full for [the 2025 list](toyota-highlander-2025-turbo.md),
  and the 5,000–50,000 pages were read here as well.
- **What differs from 2025:**
  - the standard chart's first page has a wiper blade row, so the wiper inspection is at every milestone
    (2025: from 55,000);
  - the EFI tank additive is for Hawaii, Puerto Rico and Saipan (2025 adds American Samoa);
  - the special charts repeat cells the standard chart already has (the oil change at every column, the
    air filter replacement at the 30,000s, the transmission fluid inspection at 30,000, 90,000 and
    150,000, the ball joints, boots, steering linkage and propeller shaft bolts at the 15,000s, and the
    differential and transfer case oil at the 30,000s). Such a job is in the table once, as the standard
    chart's; the table is the same as if they weren't repeated, and nothing else in the special charts
    differs from 2025.
- **The engine:** Toyota's 2026 Highlander owner's manual (`OM0E310U`, at
  `https://assets.sipb.toyota.com/publications/en/om-s/OM0E310U/pdf/OM0E310U.pdf`) gives one engine, the
  T24A-FTS 2.4-litre turbo four (2393 cm³): `displacementL` 2.4, "2.4L turbo 4-cylinder".
- Everything else is as on [the 2025 page](toyota-highlander-2025-turbo.md): no months on the milestones;
  "(if applicable)" rows (propeller shaft bolts, rear differential and transfer case oil, intercooler) in the
  table with no `service` (the owner's ruling of 2026-10-23, #33), so no rear differential or transfer case
  reminder; the combined "Engine Coolant / Intercooler Coolant (if applicable)" row split, by the same
  ruling, into "Replace engine coolant" (`coolant`) and "Replace intercooler coolant (if applicable)"
  (table only), since the mark belongs to the intercooler and every Highlander has engine coolant; the
  oil's 12 months (5,000 / 6 on dirt or dusty roads or short trips below freezing) and the coolant's 60
  months after the first change, each from its footnote.
- In the table only: the EFI tank additive, the propeller shaft bolts and the chassis nuts and bolts, and
  every inspection, including the brake booster vacuum pump at 120,000.
- Reminders: engine oil every 10,000 miles / 12 months (5,000 / 6 for severe use), tire rotation every
  5,000, cabin and engine air filters every 30,000, spark plugs every 40,000, engine coolant first at
  100,000 and then every 50,000 / 60 (its footnote's months after the first change; the first change is
  by mileage only, as on the 2025 page).
- Only for severe use (#136): automatic transmission fluid every 60,000 when towing, carrying heavy loads or
  idling long.
- Inspections are in the table only, with a check line where Glovebox has one (fluid levels, wiper blades,
  drive belts).
- Brake fluid has no replacement in the chart, so it has no reminder from this list.
- Not covered: 2027 on (no guide found on 2026-10-22).
