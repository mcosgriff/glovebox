# Volkswagen Golf SportWagen 1.8T, 2016 (Mk7)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-golf-sportwagen-2016-1.8t.json` was made. The rules it
follows are in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md).

Source: Volkswagen's 2016 "Golf and Golf SportWagen" 1.8T card (posted August 2015), filed with NHTSA as a
manufacturer communication, `2015/MC-10123075-9999`, read in full on 2026-10-29, as page images, by eye; and
Volkswagen's 2016 reissue (`USA5R506521`, printed 11/25/2025) for the oil change's time limit only.

- **One card for both bodies**, so this list is the [2016 Golf 1.8T](volkswagen-golf-2016-1.8t.md)'s, row for
  row, with the model's name; that page says how the card was read. Every Mk7 card read, and what isn't
  covered, is on the [2015 Golf 1.8T's page](volkswagen-golf-2015-1.8t.md).
- vPIC's model list for Volkswagen, 2016 (`GetModelsForMakeYear`), names it "Golf SportWagen", apart from
  "Golf", so it has a list of its own (an alias is for another name of the same car).
- **The engine**: `"engine": { "label": "1.8T", "displacementL": 1.8 }`. The 2.0L TDI has [a list of its own](volkswagen-golf-sportwagen-2016-tdi.md).
- No all-wheel-drive job: the card has none, and the 2016 SportWagen was sold with front-wheel drive only.
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; dust and pollen filter 40,000 / 24 mo; spark plugs and air filter
  60,000 / 72 mo; automatic gearbox fluid 80,000 (not for a manual); brake fluid every 24 months by time
  alone (the card says at 3 years first, then every 2).
