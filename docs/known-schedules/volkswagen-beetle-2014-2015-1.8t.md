# Volkswagen Beetle 1.8T, 2014–2015 (A5)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-beetle-2014-2015-1.8t.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Beetle card read, how the Beetle and Beetle Convertible
cards are put into one list, and what isn't covered are on the [2014 2.5L's page](volkswagen-beetle-2014-2.5.md).

| Model year | What was read | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 | The Beetle 1.8T card (posted February 2014), in full; the list was written from it | `2014/MC-10122260-9999` |
| 2014 | The Beetle Convertible 1.8T card (posted February 2014), in full: the same rows, marks and footnotes, with the two convertible roof rows | `2014/MC-10122259-9999` |
| 2014 | Volkswagen's 2014 reissue, for the oil change's time limit only (see the 2.5L's page) | reissue `USA5R505521` |
| 2015 | The Beetle 1.8T card as revised in November 2017, the newest printing, in full: row for row the 2014 card | `2017/MC-10133454-9999` |
| 2015 | The Beetle 1.8T card posted June 2014, in full: the revised card with a sunroof drain mark at 20,000 (below) | `2014/MC-10120108-9999` |
| 2015 | The Beetle Convertible 1.8T card (posted June 2014), in full: the same rows, marks and footnotes as the 2014 convertible card | `2014/MC-10120111-9999` |
| 2015 | Volkswagen's 2015 reissue, for the oil change's time limit only, as read for the [2015 Jetta Hybrid](volkswagen-jetta-2015-hybrid.md) (not read again here) | reissue `USA5R505821`, printed 11/24/2025 |

- **The card is the [2014 Beetle 2.5L](volkswagen-beetle-2014-2.5.md)'s grid with the spark plugs at 60,000 and
  120,000** (footnote, "every 6 years") in place of 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000. Every other row has the same
  marks in the same columns, the automatic gearbox's row at 50,000 and 100,000 included. It is also the
  [2015 Passat 1.8T](volkswagen-passat-2014-2015-1.8t.md)'s grid, with the convertible's roof rows.
- The convertible roof rows are table-only, named for the Beetle Convertible (see the 2.5L's page).
- `"engine": { "label": "1.8T", "displacementL": 1.8 }`.
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; tire rotation 10,000; dust and pollen filter 20,000 / 24 mo;
  automatic gearbox fluid 50,000 (not for a manual); spark plugs and air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; brake fluid
  every 24 months by time alone (the card says at 3 years first, then every 2).
- **Oil 12 months**, from Volkswagen's 2014 and 2015 reissues (the owner's ruling of 2026-10-28, #517). The table has none.
- Left out: the dealer's service sticker, the empty timing belt row, and the grey "Carefree Maintenance" shading.
- **2015: the November 2017 revision wins** (the owner's ruling of 2026-10-29 on #553,
  [part A](https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox/issues/553#issuecomment-6511794772): the newest printing wins for the 2013–2016 per-model cards). The June 2014 Beetle card draws
  "Sunroof: Open sunroof and check front water drains and clean if necessary (if applicable)" at 20,000 as well
  as at 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000; the convertible's card doesn't. NHTSA's index says of the revision
  "Removed maintenance check at 20K for 'Sunroof: Open sunroof and check water drains and clean if necessary (if
  applicable)'", and the revised card is row for row the 2014 one. So the Beetle and convertible cards of 2015
  agree as in 2014, and the list is 2014–2015. Until 2026-10-29 it was 2014 only.
- **2016** has [its own list](volkswagen-beetle-2016-1.8t.md).
