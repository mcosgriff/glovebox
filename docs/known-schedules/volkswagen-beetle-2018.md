# Volkswagen Beetle, 2018 (A5)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-beetle-2018.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Beetle card read, and what isn't covered, is on the
[2014 2.5L's page](volkswagen-beetle-2014-2.5.md).

Source: Volkswagen's 2018 "Maintenance Schedule" card for all its models except the e-Golf, filed with NHTSA
as a manufacturer communication: the newest revision, revised 11/26/2019, `2019/MC-10168646-0001`, read in
full on 2026-10-29, as page images, by eye. The earlier revisions weren't read again; the
[2018 GTI's page](volkswagen-gti-2018.md) says what they print differently.

- **The card is read as the [2018 Jetta's page](volkswagen-jetta-2018.md#how-the-2018-all-model-card-is-read)
  says.** The rows are the [2018 Jetta](volkswagen-jetta-2018.md) list's, in its wording, with one more:
  "Convertible top: Clean and lubricate locking element. Perform leak test (Beetle Convertible only)", in the
  Extended service, so at 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000: "Lubricate convertible top latch and leak test (Beetle
  Convertible)", table-only. vPIC names the convertible "Beetle" too (see the 2.5L's page), so the row is kept
  by its own words and named for the convertible; a Beetle coupe owner unticks it.
- Left out by their own words: the hood latch ("Does not apply to: Passat, Golf, Beetle, and Jetta"), the
  trailer hitch, the sunroof water drains (Passat only), the 1.4L Jetta's belt checks, the 3.6L's spark plugs and
  the 7-speed DSG (Golf R only). The AWD clutch fluid is left out: the Beetle was sold with front-wheel drive only.
- **Both gearbox rows are left out**: "Transmission, 6-Speed DSG: Change fluid and filter" and "Transmission,
  Automatic (non-DSG): Change fluid" name no model, and the card doesn't say which a Beetle has
  ([#443's question](https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox/issues/443#issuecomment-6488582333)).
- **No engine.** The spark plug row for the "1.4L, 1.8L, and 2.0L" covers every Beetle engine of the year, so the
  list names none.
- vPIC's model list for Volkswagen, 2018 (`GetModelsForMakeYear`, read 2026-10-29), names it "Beetle".
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; additional items: air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; brake fluid every 24
  months by time alone (3 years after registration first, in its name); dust and pollen filter 40,000 / 24
  mo; front axle differential lock fluid every 3 years "(if fitted)"; panoramic sunroof rails every 40,000 or
  2 years, first at 40,000 (the card's "or 3 years" for the first time is recorded, not put into months); the
  tire filler bottle's date every 2 years "(if fitted)"; spark plugs 40,000 / 48 mo.
