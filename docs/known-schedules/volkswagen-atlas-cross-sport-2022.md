# Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, 2022

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2022.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Atlas Cross Sport card read, and what isn't covered, is on the
[2020–2021 page](volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2020-2021.md#every-atlas-cross-sport-card-read).

Source: Volkswagen's "2022 VW Maintenance Schedule - All Model Gasoline Vehicles", the newest revision, revised
7/21/2021, `2021/MC-10199495-0001`, filed with NHTSA, read in full on 2026-10-29, as page images, by eye.

- **The list is the [2022 Atlas](volkswagen-atlas-2022.md)'s, row for row**, with the model's name: the card's only
  row that names either is the spark plugs, every 80,000 miles or 6 years, "Only: Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Golf GTI,
  Jetta, Jetta GLI, Taos, Tiguan" (80,000 / 72 months). The trailer hitch row names no model on this card ("(if
  equipped)"), so it is kept, "(if fitted)", as for every model.
- **The AWD clutch fluid** "(if equipped)": "Change AWD clutch fluid (AWD)", every 36 months by time alone, in
  `allWheelDriveOnly` (sold with front-wheel drive and with 4Motion: NHTSA's 2020 and 2025 safety-rating lists); the
  reading waits on [#437's question 5](https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox/issues/437#issuecomment-6491507764).
- **No gearbox row** ([#444's question 3](https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox/issues/444#issuecomment-6489685514)).
  No months on the columns; oil 10,000 or 12 months (#517).
- vPIC's model list for Volkswagen, 2022 (read 2026-10-29), names it "Atlas Cross Sport". No engine is named.
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; additional items: air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; AWD clutch fluid every 36 months
  by time alone; brake fluid every 24 months by time alone (3 years after registration first, in its name); dust and
  pollen filter 40,000 / 24 mo; spark plugs 80,000 / 72 mo.
