# Toyota RAV4 Hybrid, 2018

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/toyota-rav4-2018-hybrid.json` was made. The rules it follows are in
[known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md).

Source: Toyota's Warranty and Maintenance Guide for the 2018 RAV4 Hybrid,
`https://assets.sia.toyota.com/publications/en/omms-s/T-MMS-18RAV4HV/pdf/T-MMS-18RAV4HV.pdf`. Read on
2026-10-23.

- The 2017 Hybrid guide's log was read in full by eye ([toyota-rav4-2017-hybrid](toyota-rav4-2017-hybrid.md)),
  and the 2018 log (the same 24 milestones to 120,000 miles or 144 months) compared with it box by box; the
  boxes that differ were read on the page. One job is new: **cleaning the hybrid battery cooling intake filter**
  under extensive idling or low-speed driving, in every box from 5,000 to 120,000. It is a reminder every
  5,000 / 6 (`hybrid-battery-filter`), only for severe use. Everything else is as in 2017, apart from a new
  footnote on checking sealed units for leaks, which isn't a job.
- So the list has a year of its own, with everything the 2017 page says: `"powertrain": "hybrid"`, no drive
  marks, the same reminders and severe-use jobs, and **no reminder for the inverter coolant** (its footnote puts
  the first change at 150,000, past the table; the 100,000 job is "Replace engine coolant").
- Not covered: 2016 and 2017 (their own lists); 2019 on (a new generation).
