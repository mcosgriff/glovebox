# Volkswagen Golf SportWagen 1.8T, 2015 (Mk7)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-golf-sportwagen-2015-1.8t.json` was made. The rules it
follows are in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md).

Source: Volkswagen's 2015 "Golf and Golf SportWagen" 1.8T card (posted December 2014), filed with NHTSA as a
manufacturer communication, `2014/MC-10242450-9999`, read in full on 2026-10-29, as page images, by eye; and
Volkswagen's 2015 reissue (`USA5R505821`, printed 11/24/2025) for the oil change's time limit only.

- **One card for both bodies**, so this list is the [2015 Golf 1.8T](volkswagen-golf-2015-1.8t.md)'s, row for
  row, with the model's name. That page says how the card was read, which other cards were read, and what
  isn't covered.
- **Its own list, not an alias.** vPIC's model list for Volkswagen, 2015 (`GetModelsForMakeYear`, read
  2026-10-29), names the wagon "Golf SportWagen", apart from "Golf". A model alias is for another name of
  the same car, so the wagon has a list of its own.
- **The engine**: `"engine": { "label": "1.8T", "displacementL": 1.8 }`. The 2.0L TDI's card has no list.
- No all-wheel-drive job: the card has none, and the 2015 SportWagen was sold with front-wheel drive only.
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; tire rotation 10,000; dust and pollen filter 20,000 / 24 mo; spark
  plugs and air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; automatic gearbox fluid 50,000 (not for a manual); brake fluid every
  24 months by time alone (the card says at 3 years first, then every 2).
