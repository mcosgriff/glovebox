# Volkswagen GTI, 2008 (Mk5)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-gti-2008.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md); what the four Mk5 years share is on the
[2009 page](volkswagen-gti-2009.md#the-mk5-lists).

Source: **Volkswagen's reissue** of its 2008 "VW Service" schedule, `USA5R503821`, 24 pages, printed
**07.2022**, from Volkswagen's owners-literature site
(`https://ownersliterature.vw.com/owners-literature-service/v1/document/50a00bd3-879d-46a4-a440-3d66a3906ae7`).
Read by eye on 2026-10-26. **No card from the time was found** (#145's survey); the owner ruled on #145 that
the reissue may be the only source, cited as one with its print date.

- **The document covers every Volkswagen model** in the USA and Canada; only the USA part is read. This year names
  models as well as engines ("Eos, Passat, GTI, Jetta, Rabbit"; "2.0T FSI engine"). A line marked for another
  model or engine is left out; lines with no mark are kept.
- **The table:** "Service at" 10,000 to 120,000, every 10,000, and "Service every 5,000 miles". **The every-5,000
  oil change is the GTI's** (the owner's ruling on #145; the line names no engine). So the table has a stop every
  5,000 to 120,000, each carrying the every-5,000 jobs (oil, the two brake checks, the service display).
- **Reminders:** engine oil 5,000; tire rotation 10,000; DSG fluid and filter 40,000 (not for a manual car);
  air filter 60,000 / 72 months (60,000 names the GTI; 120,000 is "all models except Touareg"; the months are
  the 6-year time item's); spark plugs 60,000 (60,000 and 120,000, "2.0T FSI"); timing belt and tensioner
  roller at 110,000 (once, "2.0T FSI engine").
- **From the time-dependent list**, as reminders apart from the table (`additional`): the tire mobility kit's
  sealant bottle every 4 years, by time alone. Left out: the yearly airbag check (this year it is also in every
  column of the grid, and is kept there), the 2-year sunroof drain check, the convertible latch, and the tire
  pressure sensors' 6-year replacement, "(where applicable)" with nothing named (**needs a decision**, see the
  2009 page).
- **Brake fluid: no reminder** (the owner's ruling on #145). The rows at 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000 say "every
  3 years regardless of mileage" and name the GTI; the time-dependent list says "Brake fluid: Replace" every 2
  years. The two disagree, so the months are left off, and with no months and no distance there is nothing to
  remind by. The rows stay in the table, named "by time alone; the guide's two figures differ". Check the
  booklet in the car.
- **Dust and pollen filter: table only.** It is at 40,000 and 120,000 ("All models", "where applicable"), not at
  80,000 (checked on the page image), so it doesn't come round evenly; the 20,000/60,000/100,000 rows are the New
  Beetle's and City models'. Its 2-year time item ("if applicable", "for vehicles driven less than 40,000 miles in
  2 years") can't stand apart from a job of the table, so it is left out.
- **Gearbox:** the DSG fluid change ("where applicable", which names its gearbox), the automatic shift lock check
  and the automatic's leak, ATF and final drive checks are `manualExcluded`; the manual gearbox's oil check
  ("where applicable") is `manualOnly`. Whether Volkswagen means its "Automatic" lines for the DSG isn't said, so
  a DSG car keeps them.
- "(where applicable)" inspection rows (the 12V starter and auxiliary battery, the service display, the sunroof
  drains) are in the table as printed.
- Left out as not the GTI's: TDI jobs (water separator, fuel and particle filters, second battery, turbocharger
  rods), the New Beetle's spoiler, convertible top, brake fluid, air filter and pollen filter, the Eos's roof,
  sunroof rails and second battery, the Touareg's air suspension and air filter, the 4MOTION (BorgWarner)
  clutch, and the Canada-only 2.0L timing belt and spark plug lines.
- **Not covered:** the other Mk5 years have lists of their own, since no two years agree:
  [2006](volkswagen-gti-2006.md), [2007](volkswagen-gti-2007.md), [2009](volkswagen-gti-2009.md).
