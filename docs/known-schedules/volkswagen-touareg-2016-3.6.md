# Volkswagen Touareg 3.6L, 2016 (2nd generation)

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-touareg-2016-3.6.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Touareg card read, and what isn't covered, is on the
[2014–2015 3.6L page](volkswagen-touareg-2014-2015-3.6.md).

| Model year | What was read | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 | The Touareg 3.6L FSI Gasoline card (posted August 2015), in full, as page images; the list was written from it | `2015/MC-10123060-9999` |
| 2016 | Volkswagen's 2016 reissue, for the oil change's time limit only, as read for the [2016 Jetta Hybrid](volkswagen-jetta-2016-hybrid.md) (not read again here) | reissue `USA5R506521` |

- **The card is the [2016 CC 3.6L](volkswagen-cc-2016-3.6.md)'s grid** with the same two differences as in
  2014–2015: no Haldex row, and the sunroof water drains at every 20,000. So the list is that one's, in its
  wording, with those changes.
- **It differs from the 2014–2015 card** as every 2016 card does: the yearly airbag check, tire rotation, door
  checks, the rear window washer nozzle and the service sticker are gone, and the dust and pollen filter is
  "Every 2 years or 40,000 miles" (footnote 1), drawn at 40,000, 80,000 and 120,000.
- **No gearbox row** and no all-wheel-drive row: the card prints neither.
- Spark plugs and the air filter at 60,000 and 120,000 (footnotes 5 and 6, "every 6 years").
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; dust and pollen filter 40,000 / 24 mo; spark plugs and air filter
  60,000 / 72 mo; brake fluid every 24 months by time alone (the card says at 3 years first, then every 2).
- **Oil 12 months**, from Volkswagen's 2016 reissue (the owner's ruling of 2026-10-28, #517). The table has none.
