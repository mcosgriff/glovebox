# Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, 2026

How `app/src/main/assets/known-schedules/volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2026.json` was made. The rules it follows are
in [known-schedules.md](../known-schedules.md). Every Atlas Cross Sport card read, and what isn't covered, is on the
[2020–2021 page](volkswagen-atlas-cross-sport-2020-2021.md#every-atlas-cross-sport-card-read).

Source: **Volkswagen's reissued maintenance card** for 2026, `USA5R506821`, printed 02/2026, from its
owners-literature site (`…/v1/document/d063309f-f793-4d23-8799-a4ff5ffb3850`), read on 2026-10-29, as page images,
by eye. No card from the time was found, so the reissue is the only source (the owner's ruling of 2026-10-23).

- **The list is the [2023–2026 Atlas](volkswagen-atlas-2023-2026.md)'s, row for row**, with the model's name. The
  card's levels are the 2025 card's word for word; of its additional items, the Atlas Cross Sport is named on two:
  - **Spark plugs every 80,000 miles or 6 years**, "Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, GLI, GTI, Taos, Tiguan (RM1)":
    80,000 / 72 months. No engine is named.
  - **The automatic.** "Transmission, Automatic - Change Fluid & filter (if applicable)", every 80,000 miles, "09H:
    Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Tiguan (RM1)": "Change transmission fluid and filter (automatic only)", 80,000,
    `trans-fluid`, in `manualExcluded`, as on the Atlas's list. The 09U severe-use row doesn't name it.
- **"AWD Clutch - Change fluid"**, "All Applicable Vehicles": "Change AWD clutch fluid (AWD)", every 36 months by time
  alone, in `allWheelDriveOnly` (sold with front-wheel drive and with 4Motion: NHTSA's 2025 safety-rating list). The
  reading waits on [#437's question 5](https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox/issues/437#issuecomment-6491507764).
- Left out as on the Atlas's list: the front axle differential lock fluid, the belt starter-generator belt, the tire
  mobility kit bottle, the inspections and the Canada-only tire rotation.
- vPIC's model list for Volkswagen, 2026 (read 2026-10-29), names it "Atlas Cross Sport".
- Reminders: engine oil 10,000 / 12 mo; air filter 60,000 / 72 mo; AWD clutch fluid every 36 months by time alone;
  brake fluid every 24 months by time alone (USA: 3 years after registration first); dust and pollen filter
  40,000 / 24 mo; spark plugs 80,000 / 72 mo; automatic transmission fluid and filter 80,000 (not for a manual).
