Add Glovebox to Obtainium

On an Android phone with Obtainium installed, this opens it with Glovebox filled in, ready to install and keep up to date from its GitHub releases.

Open in Obtainium

No Obtainium yet? Get it from its releases, then come back and tap the button. Or add https://github.com/mcosgriff/glovebox in Obtainium by hand.

Before installing, you can check the APK's signing key against the fingerprint in the README.
